# Nectra organic search priorities

Research and implementation: 2 November 2026.

## What this research can establish

Public web results show commercial pages using multiflora honey, multiflora honey 500g and buy honey online language. They do not provide verified Google monthly search volumes. These are relevant search intent opportunities, not a most searched ranking. Validate actual impressions and queries in Search Console after publication; use Keyword Planner if quantitative demand estimates are needed.

| Intent | Natural phrases | Primary page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Find the brand | Nectra Honey, Nectra honey Virudhunagar | Home |
| Choose a product | multiflora honey, multiflora honey 500g, honey without added sugar | /product/signature |
| Give a gift | honey gift box, corporate honey gifts | /bulk-orders |
| Understand the product | what is multiflora honey, multifloral honey | /journal/what-is-multiflora-honey |
| Care for a jar | how to store honey, why does honey crystallise | Two dedicated Journal guides |
| Use the product | honey with curd, honey breakfast recipes, honey in coffee | Existing Journal recipes |

Keep one useful primary page for each intent. Do not create separate thin pages for each spelling or weight. Brand facts are founder supplied: own hives and packing in Virudhunagar, Malaipettai Street store, no added sugar or additives in Multiflora Honey. No NMR, organic certification, therapeutic or ranking claims added.

## Measurement and remaining external work

After deployment, inspect the homepage, product/signature and new Journal URLs in Search Console. Request indexing once where eligible. Keep the successful apex sitemap submission. Check that www redirects to HTTPS apex while preserving the path; an account level Cloudflare redirect may override Worker code and needs dashboard correction if it still sends all URLs to the homepage.

Compare the next 28 days with the previous 28: indexed pages, branded impressions/clicks, nonbrand queries, product landing clicks and WhatsApp inquiries. A WhatsApp button click is not a completed order. No ranking or indexing date is guaranteed. Add genuine hive photographs and batch details as available; do not manufacture testimonials or buy links.
